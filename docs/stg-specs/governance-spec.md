1. Purpose

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan:

- arsitektur governance STG,
- proposal lifecycle,
- validator governance interaction,
- treasury oversight,
- emergency governance,
- dan sovereign decision coordination.

---

2. Governance Objectives

Governance dirancang untuk:

- menjaga legitimasi perubahan protocol,
- memastikan transparency,
- memitigasi governance abuse,
- menjaga ecosystem coordination,
- mendukung decentralized evolution,
- dan menjaga long-term sustainability.

---

3. Governance Principles

Governance wajib:

- transparent,
- auditable,
- deterministic,
- participation-aware,
- quorum-based,
- dan recovery-capable.

---

4. Governance Scope

Governance dapat mengatur:

- consensus parameter,
- validator policy,
- treasury allocation,
- protocol upgrade,
- emergency protection,
- dan ecosystem funding.

---

5. Governance Restrictions

Governance TIDAK boleh:

- memalsukan finalized block,
- menghapus historical state,
- mem-bypass consensus finality,
- memalsukan treasury accounting,
- atau mengontrol validator signing key.

---

6. Core Governance Components

Governance layer terdiri dari:

- proposal system,
- voting mechanism,
- quorum validation,
- execution delay,
- treasury oversight,
- emergency governance,
- dan governance audit framework.

---

7. Governance Participants

Governance dapat melibatkan:

- validator,
- governance delegate,
- treasury council,
- infrastructure council,
- dan ecosystem representative.

10. Voting Principles

Voting wajib:

- auditable,
- replay-safe,
- deterministic,
- identity-aware,
- dan quorum-validated.

---

11. Quorum Policy

Governance wajib mendefinisikan:

- minimum participation,
- voting threshold,
- proposal validity,
- dan execution eligibility.

---

12. Governance Delay Protection

Execution delay digunakan untuk:

- audit review,
- malicious proposal detection,
- validator synchronization,
- dan emergency intervention.

---

13. Treasury Governance

Treasury governance wajib:

- transparent,
- allocation-controlled,
- audit-monitored,
- sustainability-aware,
- dan governance-reviewed.

---

14. Treasury Restrictions

Treasury governance TIDAK boleh:

- melakukan arbitrary minting,
- mem-bypass reserve policy,
- menghapus audit history,
- atau melakukan hidden allocation.

---

15. Emergency Governance

Emergency governance dapat digunakan untuk:

- validator isolation,
- treasury protection,
- network stabilization,
- emergency migration,
- dan infrastructure recovery.

---

16. Emergency Governance Restrictions

Emergency governance wajib:

- quorum-approved,
- audit logged,
- time-limited,
- dan recovery-reviewable.

---

17. Validator Governance Interaction

Validator dapat:

- memberikan suara,
- mengajukan proposal,
- memvalidasi governance state,
- dan mengaudit governance integrity.

---

18. Governance Replay Protection

Governance wajib melindungi terhadap:

- duplicated proposal,
- replayed voting,
- stale governance state,
- malicious execution replay,
- dan governance spoofing.

---

19. Governance Identity Policy

Governance identity wajib:

- cryptographically verifiable,
- replay-safe,
- governance-compatible,
- dan rotation-aware.

---

20. AI Governance Boundaries

STG-AI diperbolehkan:

- membaca governance telemetry,
- mendeteksi anomaly,
- menghasilkan proposal analysis,
- memberi governance recommendation.

STG-AI TIDAK diperbolehkan:

- memberikan suara otomatis,
- mengontrol treasury,
- memaksakan proposal,
- atau memegang governance authority.

---
