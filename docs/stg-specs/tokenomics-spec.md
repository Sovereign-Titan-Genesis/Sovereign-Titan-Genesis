1. Purpose

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan:

- model ekonomi STG,
- distribusi token,
- emission policy,
- validator incentive,
- treasury sustainability,
- dan long-term ecosystem balance.

---

2. Economic Objectives

Tokenomics dirancang untuk:

- menjaga sustainability,
- mencegah hyperinflation,
- menjaga validator participation,
- mendukung ecosystem expansion,
- menjaga governance legitimacy,
- dan memperkuat long-term reserve.

---

3. Economic Principles

Ekonomi STG wajib:

- transparent,
- auditable,
- deterministic,
- anti-abuse,
- incentive-balanced,
- dan sustainability-oriented.

---

4. Token Classification

Token Role| Purpose
Utility Token| Transaction & fees
Governance Token| Voting & governance
Validator Stake| Consensus participation
Treasury Reserve| Ecosystem reserve
Infrastructure Allocation| Development funding

---

5. Supply Model

Supply model dapat menggunakan:

- capped supply,
- adaptive emission,
- hybrid reserve emission,
- atau governance-controlled expansion.

---

6. Emission Objectives

Emission dirancang untuk:

- mendukung validator,
- menjaga network growth,
- membiayai infrastructure,
- menjaga treasury reserve,
- dan mendukung post-quantum migration.

---

Allocation Category| Purpose

---

9. Validator Reward Principles

Validator reward wajib:

- proportional,
- transparent,
- auditable,
- slashing-aware,
- dan governance-compatible.

---

10. Slashing Economic Effects

Validator yang terkena slashing dapat:

- kehilangan staking allocation,
- kehilangan reward epoch,
- mengalami temporary restriction,
- atau kehilangan validator eligibility.

---

11. Treasury Sustainability

Treasury wajib:

- menjaga reserve ratio,
- menghindari uncontrolled emission,
- memiliki emergency reserve,
- dan mendukung ecosystem continuity.

---

12. Inflation Control

Inflation policy wajib:

- predictable,
- governance-reviewed,
- rate-limited,
- dan sustainability-oriented.

---

13. Deflationary Controls

Sistem dapat mendukung:

- fee burn mechanism,
- inactive reserve recovery,
- treasury stabilization,
- dan supply balancing policy.

---

14. Governance Interaction

Governance dapat:

- memperbarui emission parameter,
- menetapkan reserve allocation,
- mengaktifkan emergency funding,
- dan mengaudit treasury balance.

Governance TIDAK boleh:

- melakukan arbitrary minting,
- memalsukan treasury state,
- atau mem-bypass audit policy.

---

15. Treasury Reserve Classes

Reserve Class| Purpose
Operational Reserve| Daily ecosystem operation
Validator Reserve| Consensus reward
Sovereign Reserve| Long-term sustainability
Emergency Reserve| Security mitigation
PQC Migration Reserve| Future cryptography upgrade

---

16. Emission Governance

Emission change wajib:

- governance-approved,
- quorum-validated,
- audit logged,
- dan execution-delayed.

---

17. Liquidity Stability Principles

Ekosistem wajib menjaga:

- reserve diversification,
- liquidity availability,
- treasury resilience,
- dan anti-shock mitigation.

---

18. Economic Abuse Protection

Sistem wajib melindungi terhadap:

- treasury drain,
- inflation abuse,
- governance manipulation,
- validator cartelization,
- dan malicious allocation.

---

19. Reward Distribution Cadence

Reward Type| Cadence
Validator Reward| Per epoch
Treasury Allocation| Periodic
Governance Incentive| Proposal-based
Emergency Allocation| Conditional

---

20. AI Economic Monitoring Boundaries

STG-AI diperbolehkan:

- membaca economic telemetry,
- mendeteksi anomaly,
- menghasilkan sustainability analysis,
- memberi governance recommendation.

STG-AI TIDAK diperbolehkan:

- mencetak token otomatis,
- memegang treasury authority,
- mengubah emission policy,
- atau melakukan transfer reserve otomatis.
