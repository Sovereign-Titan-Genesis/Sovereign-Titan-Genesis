1. Purpose

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan:

- arsitektur treasury STG,
- reserve management,
- validator funding,
- ecosystem sustainability,
- treasury governance,
- dan sovereign reserve continuity.

---

2. Treasury Objectives

Treasury dirancang untuk:

- menjaga sustainability jaringan,
- mendukung validator participation,
- membiayai infrastructure growth,
- menjaga reserve stability,
- mendukung emergency recovery,
- dan memastikan long-term ecosystem continuity.

---

3. Treasury Principles

Treasury wajib:

- transparent,
- auditable,
- allocation-controlled,
- sustainability-oriented,
- governance-reviewed,
- dan recovery-capable.

---

4. Treasury Scope

Treasury dapat digunakan untuk:

- validator incentive,
- infrastructure funding,
- ecosystem development,
- emergency recovery,
- PQC migration support,
- dan sovereign reserve maintenance.

---

5. Treasury Restrictions

Treasury TIDAK boleh:

- melakukan arbitrary minting,
- memalsukan accounting,
- mem-bypass governance,
- menghapus audit history,
- atau melakukan hidden allocation.

---

6. Treasury Architecture

Treasury layer terdiri dari:

- reserve vault,
- validator allocation engine,
- governance allocation control,
- emergency reserve mechanism,
- audit logging layer,
- dan sustainability monitoring.

---

7. Treasury Reserve Classes

Reserve Class| Purpose
Validator Reserve| Consensus reward

Governance Reserve| Governance operations
Emergency Reserve| Crisis recovery
PQC Reserve| Post-quantum migration

11. Governance Treasury Interaction

- memperbarui funding parameter,

12. Governance Restrictions

Governance TIDAK boleh:

- mengambil reserve tanpa quorum,
- memalsukan reserve accounting,
- mem-bypass audit requirement,
- atau melakukan unrestricted treasury extraction.

13. Reserve Sustainability Policy

Reserve wajib menjaga:

- long-term liquidity,
- allocation balance,
- emergency survivability,
- validator continuity,
- dan economic resilience.

---

14. Emergency Reserve Protection

Emergency reserve wajib:

- multi-signature protected,
- governance-controlled,
- audit logged,
- time-locked,
- dan recovery-monitored.

---

15. Treasury Auditability

Seluruh aktivitas treasury wajib:

- immutable,
- timestamped,
- explorer-visible,
- governance-reviewable,
- dan cryptographically verifiable.

---

16. Treasury Accounting

Treasury accounting wajib:

- deterministic,
- transparent,
- replay-safe,
- reserve-aware,
- dan sustainability-monitored.

---

17. Distribution Controls

Treasury distribution wajib:

- governance-approved,
- threshold-validated,
- audit-logged,
- allocation-limited,
- dan replay-protected.

---

18. Inflation Protection

Treasury wajib memitigasi:

- uncontrolled emission,
- reserve depletion,
- governance abuse,
- validator economic capture,
- dan long-term instability.

---

19. Treasury Replay Protection

Treasury layer wajib melindungi terhadap:

- duplicated withdrawal,
- replayed allocation,
- stale treasury state,
- malicious extraction,
- dan forged accounting event.

---

20. AI Treasury Boundaries

STG-AI diperbolehkan:

- membaca treasury telemetry,
- mendeteksi anomaly,
- menghasilkan reserve analysis,
- memberi sustainability recommendation.

STG-AI TIDAK diperbolehkan:

- memegang treasury authority,
- melakukan transfer otomatis,
- mengubah reserve state,
- atau mem-bypass governance approval.
