1. Purpose

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan:

- model tata kelola STG,
- distribusi otoritas,
- governance proposal lifecycle,
- voting legitimacy,
- emergency governance,
- dan sovereign coordination boundaries.

---

2. Governance Objectives

Governance dirancang untuk:

- menjaga legitimasi keputusan,
- mencegah governance capture,
- menjaga decentralization balance,
- menjaga ecosystem sustainability,
- dan memastikan auditability keputusan.

---

3. Governance Principles

Governance wajib:

- transparent,
- auditable,
- deterministic,
- rate-limited,
- quorum-based,
- dan resistant terhadap abuse.

---

4. Governance Components

Component| Function
Validator Council| Consensus integrity
Governance DAO| Proposal & voting
Treasury Authority| Treasury execution
Emergency Council| Crisis mitigation

7. Proposal Categories

Category| Description
Technical| Protocol upgrades
Treasury| Fund allocation
Governance| Policy changes
Security| Emergency actions
Infrastructure| Network operations

---

8. Voting Legitimacy Rules

Proposal hanya dianggap sah apabila:

- quorum tercapai,
- voting window valid,
- audit rules terpenuhi,
- execution delay selesai,
- dan proposal tidak melanggar protocol boundary.

---

9. Quorum Policy

Governance Action| Minimum Quorum
Standard Proposal| 51%
Treasury Proposal| 60%
Protocol Upgrade| 67%
Emergency Governance| 75%

---

10. Execution Delay Policy

Execution delay digunakan untuk:

- audit review,
- community visibility,
- anomaly detection,
- emergency cancellation.

---

11. Governance Authority Separation

Tidak ada satu entitas yang boleh mengontrol:

- validator consensus,
- treasury execution,
- governance voting,
- emergency override,
- dan audit verification
  secara bersamaan.

---

12. Emergency Governance

Emergency governance hanya diperbolehkan untuk:

- critical security incident,
- validator compromise,
- treasury attack,
- consensus instability,
- infrastructure collapse.

---

13. Emergency Governance Restrictions

Emergency authority wajib:

- multi-signature protected,
- time-limited,
- audit logged,
- quorum-approved,
- dan publicly reviewable.

---

14. Governance Abuse Protection

Sistem wajib memiliki perlindungan terhadap:

- governance cartelization,
- malicious proposal spam,
- treasury capture,
- validator collusion,
- emergency authority abuse.

---

15. Proposal Validation Rules

Proposal wajib:

- memiliki unique identifier,
- memiliki execution scope jelas,
- memiliki audit metadata,
- memiliki governance category,
- dan tidak melanggar protocol safety.

---

16. Treasury Governance Interaction

Treasury execution hanya boleh dilakukan apabila:

- proposal valid,
- quorum tercapai,
- execution delay selesai,
- multi-signature approval aktif,
- dan audit logging berjalan.

---

17. Validator Governance Role

Validator diperbolehkan:

- voting governance proposal,
- memberi technical review,
- memvalidasi protocol safety,
- dan mendeteksi governance anomaly.

Validator TIDAK diperbolehkan:

- melakukan unilateral treasury control,
- mem-bypass governance quorum,
- atau melakukan forced proposal execution.

---

18. Audit Council Responsibilities

Audit council bertanggung jawab untuk:

- memverifikasi governance execution,
- memverifikasi treasury integrity,
- memeriksa emergency governance,
- memvalidasi slashing fairness,
- dan menjaga transparency.

---

19. AI Governance Boundaries

STG-AI diperbolehkan:

- membaca governance events,
- melakukan proposal analysis,
- mendeteksi anomaly,
- memberi recommendation,
- menghasilkan governance risk score.

STG-AI TIDAK diperbolehkan:

- melakukan voting otomatis,
- mengeksekusi proposal,
- memegang treasury authority,
- atau melakukan governance override.

---

20. Governance Transparency Rules

Semua governance activity wajib:

- immutable,
- publicly reviewable,
- timestamped,
- explorer-visible,
- machine-parseable.

---

21. Governance Security Controls

Governance wajib mendukung:

- proposal rate limiting,
- spam prevention,
- quorum integrity verification,
- replay protection,
- execution rollback policy.

---

22. Governance Recovery Model

Dalam kondisi darurat:

- emergency governance dapat aktif,
- treasury freeze dapat dilakukan,
- validator suspension dapat diterapkan,
- dan recovery proposal dapat diprioritaskan.

---

23. Sovereign Governance Boundaries

Sovereign layer diperbolehkan:

- melakukan monitoring,
- risk analysis,
- infrastructure coordination.

Sovereign layer TIDAK diperbolehkan:

- mem-bypass public governance,
- menghapus audit trail,
- mengendalikan voting outcome secara langsung.

---

24. Threat Model

Dokumen ini dirancang untuk memitigasi:

- governance capture,
- malicious quorum manipulation,
- treasury abuse,
- emergency governance exploitation,
- validator collusion,
- proposal spam attack,
- dan centralized authority dominance.

---

25. Post-Quantum Governance Readiness

Governance architecture wajib mendukung:

- future cryptographic migration,
- hybrid signature governance,
- distributed identity verification,
- dan cryptographic agility.

---

26. Status

Dokumen ini merupakan draft spesifikasi governance architecture untuk:

- STG ecosystem governance,
- sovereign coordination,
- treasury legitimacy,
- validator governance,
- dan long-term decentralized sustainability.
